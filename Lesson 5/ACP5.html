Facts about Cats

Cats are highly specialized, independent hunters with 30 teeth, 18 toes, and unique nose prints, often sleeping 14–16 hours daily.

They navigate using sensitive whiskers that detect airflow, can jump up to 6 times their length, and use purring for comfort, sometimes reaching volumes similar to a shower.
