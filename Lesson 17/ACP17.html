🐠 Clownfish

Shallow Reefs

Clownfish live among venomous sea anemones. The anemone protects the fish, and the fish cleans the anemone!

🐢 Sea Turtle

Open Ocean

Sea turtles have been swimming in our oceans for over 100 million years, traveling massive distances to lay eggs.

🦈 Great White Shark

Coastal Waters

Great whites are the world's largest predatory fish. They have an incredible sense of smell to track food.

🦑 Giant Squid

Abyssal Deep

This giant mollusk lives in extreme ocean depths. Its eyes can be as large as basketballs to see in the dark.

🐋 Blue Whale

Global Oceans

The largest animal ever known to live on Earth. Their hearts are the size of a small car!

🦦 Sea Otter

Kelp Forests

Otters float on their backs to rest, eat, and sleep. They often hold hands so they don't float away.
