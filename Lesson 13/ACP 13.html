Grid:

Grids are structural frameworks of intersecting lines used to organize content, align elements, and create consistent, balanced layouts in design, mapping, and engineering. They ensure visual consistency in print, facilitate responsive, adaptable layouts in web design, and improve structural stability.

Width Grid:

Width grids (or grid layouts) are structural, responsive design systems that divide web pages into columns, rows, and gaps (gutters) to align content consistently. Usually featuring 12, 8, or 4 columns, they ensure elements scale properly across desktop, tablet, and mobile, with grid width determining the maximum container

Column Grids:

Column grids are structural frameworks in graphic and web design that divide a layout into vertical fields (columns) to align, organize, and stack content, such as text and images. They are crucial for creating consistent, balanced, and responsive layouts, often enabling content to reflow seamlessly across mobile, tablet, and desktop screen
